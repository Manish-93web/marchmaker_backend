      Marchmaker Live Test Guide

      Marchmaker makes a small number of human-reviewed marriage introductions in India. This guide walks you through the whole journey on the live test site, from creating two members to a finished introduction and chat, with every step an admin must do in between.

      Start here Overview

      You need nothing installed. Two web addresses and one admin login are enough.

      What the platform does

      • Members register, fill a profile, upload photos, verify their ID and say what they are looking for.
      • The admin team reviews every profile, photo and ID, and approves every match by hand.
      • Introductions go to both people. Each says yes or no. A credit is spent only when both say yes, and then messaging opens.
      • There is no swiping and no browsing of other members.

      Read these first

      This is a test site
      • Use made-up details. Do not upload real ID documents or photos of real people. Use any sample image for an ID.
      • Payments run in Stripe test mode. No real money moves, and only Stripe's test card works (given in section 8).
      • The admin login is shared and has full power. Please do not change its password or delete other people's accounts.
      Keep your sign-ins apart
      • Use a separate private (incognito) window for each member you sign in as. Two members in one normal window log each other out.
      • The admin console keeps its own sign-in, so you can stay signed in to it and to one member at the same time. Open it in its own tab or window.

      Still as Member one. On the dashboard press Continue your profile. The wizard saves as you type, so there is no Save button. Look for “Saved” near the top of the form.

      Fill in the steps in order with Next and Back. You can leave and come back later. The wizard reopens at the first unfinished step.

      2.2 Upload photos

      1. In the Photos step choose one or two clear pictures of a face. They must be JPEG, PNG or WebP, at least 200 × 200 pixels and no larger than 10 MB. The first photo is the main one.
      2. The photo first shows Processing…. Result: within about two minutes it changes to Awaiting review. The site is resizing the picture and removing hidden location data.
      Still “Processing…” after 5 minutes?

      Reload the page once. If it is still stuck, tell the owner and say which member it was.

      2.3 Review and submit

      1. Open the last step, Review and submit. It lists any step that is still missing.
      2. Press Submit for review. Result: “Submitted for review. Our team is reading your profile now. This usually takes up to 48 hours.” The profile is locked until the admin answers.

      3. Admin: approve the profile and photos Part C

      Now play the admin. Open the admin address in a different browser window from your members.

      3.1 Sign in to the admin console

      1. Open https://marchmakeradmin.vercel.app.
      2. Sign in with admin@example.com and Admin@12345. Result: the console opens with a menu down the left. Admin accounts normally need an authenticator code. If it asks for one on this test site, tell the owner.

      3.2 Approve the profile

      1. In the left menu choose Profile review. Rohan's profile is in the queue.
      2. Open it and read every section.
      3. Press Approve (shortcut a). To send it back instead press Request changes (c), pick the steps to revisit and write a short note. Result: the status becomes approved. On the member side the dashboard moves on to verification.

      3.3 Approve the photo

      1. Choose Photo review in the menu.
      2. Each pending photo has Approve and Reject buttons. Reject asks for a reason. Approve it. Result: the photo leaves the queue. Members only ever see approved photos, and other members see them blurred until an introduction allows more.

      Also try Request changes once on a profile, check that the member sees your note, fix it as the member and submit again.

      4. ID verification Part D

      This opens only after the profile is approved. No outside ID service is connected, so a person at the admin console checks the pictures by hand.

      4.1 Member uploads the documents

      1. As Rohan, refresh the dashboard and press Verify your identity (or open /verification).
      2. Choose the ID type (Aadhaar masked, PAN card, Passport, Driving licence or Voter ID) and enter the last 4 characters of the number. The full number is never asked for. Make up the characters.
      3. Upload the front of the ID, then the back. Use any JPEG, PNG or WebP picture from 2 KB to 8 MB. It does not have to be a real document. Result: each box shows a green “done” mark. (The file chooser goes back to “No file chosen”. That is normal. Look for the green mark.)
      4. Selfie. Press Open camera, or Upload a photo instead if there is no camera.
      5. When all three show done, press Submit for verification. Result: the page says your verification is being reviewed.

      4.2 Admin reviews

      1. In the admin console open Verification and open Rohan's case.
      2. Look at the front, back and selfie. Tick the checklist items you agree with: name matches the profile, date of birth matches, selfie matches the document photo, document is not expired, document looks genuine.
      3. Press Approve. You can also Reject or ask for a re-upload of specific items. Result: on the member side the ID badge is earned and the journey moves to Matching. Every view of an ID image by staff is logged.

      5. Preferences and assessments Part E

      The matching engine needs to know what each person looks for.

      5.1 Set preferences

      1. As Rohan open the dashboard and press Your preferences (/preferences).
      2. Keep it wide so a match is likely: age 18 to 35, Religion Hindu, Mother tongue Hindi, Diet Vegetarian.
      3. For each, choose whether it is a must-have or flexible, and how much it matters (1 to 3). A must-have removes anyone who fails it. A flexible one only changes the score. Result: an estimate of how many people fit appears. Saving keeps it.
      Use flexible preferences while testing

      Every must-have is a hard filter. A strict one, or one that depends on spelling (like a city), can silently remove the only other person in your test and give you no match at all.

      5.2 Assessments (optional)

      On the dashboard open Assessments and finish one. The answers add to the match score. Skipping them is fine.

      6. Member two Part F

      A match needs another person. Repeat sections 1 to 5 for a second member in another private window.

      1. Register as a woman: first name Pooja, date of birth 5 May 2002, a different email address, and password Passw0rd-Test2. Confirm her email from the link (section 1).
      2. Profile: fill it with matching details: Hindu, Hindi, Vegetarian, a city and state, and a bio of 50 or more characters. Upload a photo and submit (section 2).
      3. Admin: approve her profile and her photo (section 3).
      4. Verification: upload ID and selfie, then approve it in the admin console (section 4).
      5. Preferences: age 20 to 40, Hindu, Hindi, Vegetarian, all flexible (section 5). Result: both members now show journey Matching on their dashboards.

      7. Matching Part G

      The system suggests pairs, then a person checks and approves each one. Nothing reaches a member before that.

      7.1 How a pair is chosen

      • Who is eligible: an active member with an approved profile, at least one approved photo and an approved ID.
      • Hard filters first: every must-have, in both directions, plus blocks and pairs already rejected.
      • Score from 0 to 1 from three parts: how well each meets the other's preferences (35%), assessments (30%) and life plans such as timeline, children and living arrangements (35%).
      • Cut-off: pairs below 0.55 are not suggested. Members never see the number. They see plain reasons such as “same city”.

      7.2 Make the wait short (do this first)

      After an admin approves a match there is an undo window before it reaches the members. It is 10 minutes by default. For a quick test make it short.

      1. In the admin console open Matching settings (the last menu item).
      2. In the Review card set Undo window (minutes) to 0 and save.

      7.3 Run matching

      1. Open Match review and press Run matching now. Result: a summary of how many suggestions were created. Rohan and Pooja should appear as a pair with a score.
      No suggestion for your two members?
      • Loosen both members' preferences. Make sure nothing is a must-have that the other person does not meet.
      • Check both have an approved profile, photo and ID. Without all three they are not in the pool.
      • The score may be under 0.55. Finish an assessment on both, or lower Minimum fit to suggest in Matching settings.
      • Or skip the engine: on the Match review page press Manual introduction. You need each member's ID, which is the long code at the end of their page address under Members.

      7.4 Review and approve the match

      1. Open the Rohan and Pooja suggestion.
      2. Read both profiles, the reasons, and the “why we chose them” text. You can edit that text. It is checked for things that are not allowed, such as contact details.
      3. Press Approve (shortcut a) and confirm. Other choices are Reject (r), Snooze (s) and Escalate (e). Result: the match is approved. After the undo window it is released to both members. With the window at 0 this takes about a minute or two.

      Some pairs need two different approvers, for example when a member has a past suspension. The page says so when that applies.

      8. Plans and credits (test payment) Part H

      8.1 Pay with the test card

      1. As Rohan open Account, then Billing (/billing).
      2. Choose a plan, for example Premium, press its button, or under Or buy single introductions press Buy on a pack. If it asks for a state for tax, choose any state.
      3. You are taken to a Stripe payment page. Enter these test details:

      4. Press Pay or Subscribe. Result: you return to Billing, which says “Confirming your payment…”. Then your credits show, and your plan shows as active. An invoice appears in the billing history.
      5. Do the same as Pooja.
      Credits not showing?

      Reload Billing after a minute. The site also checks payments on its own every 10 minutes. If nothing has appeared after 15 minutes, tell the owner.

      Open the invoice from the history. It is a printable tax invoice with the GST split and the rupee sign.

      9. Introduction and chat Part I

      Use each member's own private window.

      9.1 Say yes

      1. As Rohan open Your introductions (/introductions). You see one card: the other person's name, age, city and profession, why you were matched, and a blurred photo. A bell notification may also show.
      2. Press Say yes. Pressing Not for me would ask for a reason instead. Result: “You said yes. We will tell you when they respond.” One credit is held, not spent. It comes straight back if the other person declines or does not reply in 7 days.
      3. As Pooja open her introductions and press Say yes too. Result: the introduction shows You are connected. Both credits are now spent, and more of each profile and photo unlocks.
      “You need an introduction credit”?

      You have no credit. Complete the payment in section 8, wait for the credit to show on Billing, then try again.

      9.2 Message each other

      1. Open Messages (/messages) as either member and send a first note. It has a minimum and maximum length.
      2. Switch to the other member and reply. Result: your messages appear in the conversation for both people. Contact details are not shared automatically.

      Also try, on the introduction, Report and Block (these go to the admin Moderation queue), and the feedback requests the site sends later.

      9.3 Watch it from the admin side

      • Introductions monitor shows every introduction and its state.
      • Payments, Subscriptions and Invoices list the test purchases.
      • Audit log records who approved what and when.

      13. How to report a problem Help

      For each problem, please send the owner:

      • which site (member or admin) and the page address,
      • which member you were signed in as,
      • what you pressed, what you expected and what happened,
      • the time it happened (so it can be found in the logs), and a screenshot if you can.
