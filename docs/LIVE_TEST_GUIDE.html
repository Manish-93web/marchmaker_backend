      How to use Marchmaker

      Purple steps are done by the admin. The rest are done by members. You need two members (one woman, one man), so you will sign up twice.

      Two tips before you begin
      • Use a private (incognito) window for each member, and a different window for the admin. Otherwise the sign-ins mix up.
      • Use made-up details and any sample picture for photos and ID. This is a test site.

      3. Admin: approve the profile and photo Admin

      Open the admin website in a different window.

      1. Go to https://marchmakeradmin.vercel.app and sign in with admin@example.com and Admin@12345.
      2. In the left menu press Profile review, open Rohan's profile, read it and press Approve.
      3. In the left menu press Photo review and press Approve on his photo. You will see: the items leave the waiting list. On the member website, Rohan's Dashboard now asks him to verify his identity.

      4. Verify the ID Member + Admin

      As Member one

      1. On the Dashboard press Verify your identity.
      2. Choose an ID type (for example PAN card) and type any 4 characters for the last 4 characters of the number.
      3. Upload a picture for the front and one for the back. Any sample picture works. Each box shows a green “done” mark.
      4. For the selfie press Open camera, or Upload a photo instead.
      5. Press Submit for verification.

      As the admin

      1. In the left menu press Verification and open Rohan's case.
      2. Look at the pictures, tick the checklist items and press Approve. You will see: Rohan's Dashboard now shows Matching.

      5. Set preferences Member

      1. As Rohan, on the Dashboard press Your preferences.
      2. Set a wide range so a match is likely: age 18 to 35, religion Hindu, mother tongue Hindi, diet Vegetarian.
      3. Mark each one as flexible (not “must-have”), then save.

      6. Do the same for Member two Member + Admin

      Open another private window. Member two is a woman.

      1. Sign up (step 1) with first name Pooja, I am A woman, date of birth 5 May 2002, a different email address and password Passw0rd-Test2. Confirm her email.
      2. Fill the profile (step 2) with Hindu, Hindi, Vegetarian, a city and state, a bio of 50 or more characters and a photo. Submit it.
      3. Admin approves her profile and photo (step 3).
      4. Verify her ID and let the admin approve it (step 4).
      5. Set her preferences (step 5): age 20 to 40, Hindu, Hindi, Vegetarian, all flexible. You will see: both Rohan and Pooja show Matching on their Dashboards.

      7. Admin: match the two members Admin

      1. In the admin website press Matching settings (last item in the left menu). In the Review box set Undo window (minutes) to 0 and save. This makes the match arrive quickly.
      2. Press Match review, then Run matching now. Rohan and Pooja should appear as a pair. Not appearing? Make both members' preferences wider and check both were approved in steps 3 and 4. You can also press Manual introduction to pair them yourself.
      3. Open the pair, read the details and press Approve, then confirm. You will see: after a minute or two, an introduction appears on both members' Dashboards and under Your introductions.

      8. Add a credit (test payment) Member

      To say yes to an introduction a member needs one credit. The payment is a test, so no real money is used. Do this for both Rohan and Pooja.

      1. Press Account, then Billing.
      2. Choose a plan, for example Premium, or press Buy on a single introduction pack. Choose any state if asked.
      3. On the payment page enter the test card:
        FieldEnter

        CVCAny 3 digits, for example 123
        Name and emailAnything
      4. Press Pay or Subscribe. You will see: you return to Billing, it says “Confirming your payment…”, and then your credits appear.

      9. Say yes and chat Member

      1. As Rohan press Your introductions. You see Pooja's card and why you were matched.
      2. Press Say yes. You will see: “You said yes. We will tell you when they respond.”
      3. As Pooja open Your introductions and press Say yes too. You will see: “You are connected”.
      4. Press Messages, write a first note and send it. Switch to the other member and reply. You will see: the messages in the conversation for both people. The whole journey is complete.

      If you get stuck Help

      • No confirmation email: check spam and wait a few minutes.
      • A photo still says Processing…: reload the page after a few minutes.
      • No match appears: make both members' preferences wider, or use Manual introduction in Match review.
      • “You need an introduction credit”: finish step 8 for that member and wait for the credit to show on Billing.
      • The admin asks for a code: tell the person who sent you this guide.
